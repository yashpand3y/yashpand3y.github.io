About

With a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Drexel University—concentrating in Game Programming and Artificial Intelligence—I bring over a decade of programming expertise to the table. My strong mathematical foundation heavily influences my analytical, systems-level approach to solving complex engineering challenges.

Throughout my career across various data engineering, software, and AI roles, my core responsibility has remained consistent: solving multifaceted problems. From optimizing high-stakes databases to architecting cloud data pipelines, I focus on executing work that is scalable, efficient, and well-designed. I have also embraced leadership roles, serving as the Programming Team Lead for Rushdown, Vice Captain of the Drexel Fencing Team (Épée), and spearheading several academic and technical projects.

You can find a detailed breakdown of my professional experience here.

  • What is my story so far?

    • Born in New Delhi, India, in 1999, my life has been a global journey spanning multiple countries and cultures. I spent my formative years in India, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates, before moving to the United States (2017 – December 2024) to earn my degree. More recently, I spent a year working in India's tech hub, Bengaluru (March 2025 – April 2026), and am currently based in Vaishali, Delhi NCR (April 2026 – Present). Transitioning between diverse environments has deeply shaped my holistic approach to life and engineering, cultivating strong traits like adaptability, flexibility, and tenacity.

    I completed my 9th and 10th grades at Delhi Private School - Dubai before transferring to Delhi Public School - Noida to finish high school. Despite the challenges of transferring schools across countries, I put in the work to earn the A.J. Drexel Scholarship, which allowed me to attend Drexel University.

  • What got me into programming?

    • My affinity for programming began in the 6th grade. By high school, I was building a foundation in Object-Oriented Programming (Turbo C++) and SQL. Driven by an innate passion for designing interacting systems, I created my first 2D puzzle game, Overlap.

    Developing Overlap pushed me far beyond the standard syllabus, teaching me how to leverage graphics libraries and optimize complex logic. Programming games is similar to designing large systems and ensuring they all interact precisely as intended—skills that naturally translated into designing large-scale software architectures.

  • Why did I choose to study what I did?

    • Motivated by early successes in game development, I pursued Computer Science at Drexel University. My drive for excellence led me to incorporate an artificial intelligence track, completing nearly two terms' worth of extra coursework.

    Out of a deep interest in continuous learning, I took classes in Mathematics, Open Data Research, Animation, Game Art, Photography, Philosophy, and Psychology. I excelled in my core computer science classes, eventually becoming a teaching assistant for CS281 (Systems Architecture) and CS375 (Web Development).

  • What do I like outside of class?

    • Outside of engineering, I love traveling, photography, writing, working out, and playing sports. After playing football and tennis in high school, I challenged myself to learn fencing at Drexel. Through rigorous dedication, I secured 3rd place individually at the BWCFC championships, helping the team secure 2nd place in the Épée category. Beyond fencing, I am also a member of Drexel Speaks and the Drexel E-Sports and Gaming Association.

    I am also deeply interested in data activism. During a research project analyzing US Census Demographics Data, I utilized Python and Pandas to highlight evidence of Redlining in Philadelphia, demonstrating how minority and lower-income populations remain concentrated near industrial and traffic-heavy zones.

  • Looking Ahead

    • As I look to the future, my ambitions span academia, athletics, and entrepreneurship. I am actively seeking higher education opportunities for a Master's degree in Europe to further deepen my technical expertise and research capabilities. Athletically, I am training rigorously to compete both nationally and internationally in Épée fencing. Ultimately, my long-term vision is to merge my technical acumen, creative drive, and leadership experience to open my own independent game studio, developing innovative and systems-driven experiences.
